Document view

BuildingOptionalTools

Shows one source document in its own order, with the excerpt the reader came for marked, steps between its excerpts, and a question about the document for the thread.

Use it when

  • Users need to read a source in context, not just the excerpt.
  • A footnote or an excerpt in Sources should open the document at that excerpt, and lead back.

Variants

  • The excerpts only, as long as genki has no route for a document's whole text: the document's retrieved excerpts in its own order, by position, with «[…]» where the positions say text is left out, a sentence saying so, and «Les dokumentet på Kudos». Without positions, the service's order, and no gap is claimed.
  • Footnotes that name excerpts: the cited excerpts are «Utdrag N» with their links back beside the title, numbered by the whole answer as in Sources. Footnotes that name only documents: nothing is numbered.
  • A target that names an excerpt marks it, scrolls to it and focuses it. One that names only the document lands on its title and marks nothing until the reader steps.

The document reads as its card in Sources opened in full: the same head, body and excerpt boxes, cleaned and drawn by the same Markdown renderer, and the same mark on the excerpt the reader is on.

Props

  • sources and target: the answer's sources[], as Sources takes them, and which document to show and where. A footnote's target, or the excerpt the reader chose in Sources. Each new target, by identity, is followed once. onTargetMissing reports one the sources do not hold.
  • citations, onShowUse, citationHref, answerIndex and answerCount: the footnotes and the links back, exactly as in Sources, so «Utdrag 2» is the same excerpt in both.
  • onReturn and returnTo: back to where the reader came from, by the button at the top or Escape on the document. «Tilbake til svaret» by default, «Tilbake til kildene» with sources.
  • onAsk: «Spør om dette dokumentet». The block hands the host the question, the document and the excerpt the reader stands on; the host asks it in the thread. Without it, no field is drawn.
  • corpus names the collection in «Les dokumentet på Kudos». disclaimer (on by default) says the text is quoted, not generated. title is the block's heading, «Dokumentvisning», or false for none. headingLevel is the document title's level, 2 to 5, 3 by default.
  • color and headerColor set Designsystemet's data-color on the block and on the document's head, each only when given. className is added to the block's own; children come after the document.

When genki can give a document's chunks in order, they come as one more optional prop beside sources, and the excerpts are marked among them. Nothing above changes.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. Three are pinned on purpose, as in Sources: the mark on the excerpt the reader is on to accent, and the quiet notes to neutral. The steps between excerpts stay at the top while the document scrolls, on the body's own surface.

What we have learned

  • Users need to see the passage and the sources behind a claim, and make their own judgement.
  • Users should be able to move between the excerpts in a document.
  • genki has no route for a document's whole text, or all of its chunks: the frontend contract gives only the excerpts an answer retrieved. The view says so, and links to the original.
  • A target that names only the document cites no excerpt, so none is marked: marking the first would read as the answer citing it.
  • Documents are never numbered (no «Kilde 2 av 4»): footnotes are numbered by excerpt.

Open questions

  • Should a question asked in the document search only that document? genki can scope a run to one document inside, but /v1/chat/completions takes no such field.
  • The PDF page itself, with the passage marked on it, needs pages from genki.
