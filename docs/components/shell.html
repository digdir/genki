Components / Shell

Shell

BuildingHolds the others

Three columns, as in Visual Studio Code: a primary panel, the main column and a secondary panel. The panels are slots: the shell knows nothing of threads or sources. A panel folds to a rail, is resized by dragging, clicking or the keys, and becomes a drawer over the main column in a narrow window. The shell reports every change; the app stores it.

Use it when

  • An assistant has more than the conversation: threads, a filter, sources.
  • The reader should keep the answer and its sources side by side, and choose how wide each is.

Don't use it when

  • The assistant is only a conversation. Leave both panels out, or use no shell.
  • The app has a page layout of its own, such as genki's portal.

Variants

  • Three columns: both panels, as kunnskapsassistenten.
  • One panel: leave primary or secondary out. The breakpoints follow.
  • The top row, the standard below 774: both rails stand in one row above the main column (kunnskapsassistenten#120). compactBar={false} keeps them at the sides.
  • Cards, frame="cards", the design's dev-navbar board: each panel and the main column is a rounded card on the page's ground, 16 from the window and 12 apart, with no top bar. A rail is a card of 76 with the same gap, and the panel's button is a 44 icon. Not in the top row, which stays as it is. Flush, the default, is what kunnskapsassistenten draws today.

Props

  • primary and secondary: each a panel, with label (its name, after what is in it: «Tråder og filter»), children (what scrolls), head (controls on the panel's row, beside «Skjul»), pinned (pinned to the top of what scrolls; the shell pads the scrolling region as tall as it, from the moment the panel opens, so a block that scrolls to its target there does not land under it, and sets that height on the region as --uib-shell-pinned-block-size, for a block that pins its own head under it; the main column's mainPinned does the same), pinnedRef (that region's element, for a block that draws its own head there, such as the filter's pinIn; a stable function), foot and footPinned, railFoot (what the folded panel shows at its bottom, such as the reader's avatar; not in the top row), rail (what the folded panel shows under its button, such as «Ny tråd», dev-navbar, on a drawer's rail too; not in the top row), title (the panel's visible name, an h2 on its row: «Tråder», «Verktøy»), size (width, minWidth, maxWidth), defaultCollapsed and landmark. A host may change a panel's size while the page is open, as the next look's primary panel goes from the threads with their filter to the settings menu: the panel is then drawn within its new floor and ceiling, and the width it had comes back with its old size.
  • companion: a second column in the panel's card, towards the main column, as the dev boards draw the filter beside the threads (dev-filter-panel). It has its own title, a subtitle under it, its children, which scroll on their own, and its width; its label names its region. The panel's size counts both columns, and the panel's own column keeps its width less the companion's. With keepWidth it is the other way round: the companion keeps its width, and the panel's own column takes every resize, so the panel's size.minWidth is the own column's floor plus the companion's width. The panel's button stands on the companion's row, at the card's edge, and folds both. In a drawer the companion stands under the panel's own column.
  • children and mainPinned: the main column, and what stays at its top. mainTitle: the page's h1, such as the thread's title, pinned at the top of the main column with a line under it; in the cards frame it spans the card. Every title the shell draws looks alike, Heading xs on one line cut with «…», and its text stands on the same line in every panel. mainSize: its floor and reading width, 640 and 800.
  • frame: flush, the default, or cards. inset: the cards' margin from the window, 16; it must match --genai-shell-inset.
  • initialLayout and onLayoutChange: which panels are folded, the widths the reader moved, and whether the reader shut a panel. The shell never stores anything; the app does, and passes it back.
  • request: open a panel the reader asked for, such as by a footnote; offer one that has new content, such as an answer with sources; or close-drawers, such as after choosing a thread. A new object is acted on once.
  • compactBar: the top row below 774, on by default. false keeps the rails at the sides on a phone.
  • skipLinks: more skip links after «Hopp til hovedinnhold», such as «Hopp til skrivefeltet».
  • frameWidth: drawn in a host's preview of that width, as the portal's builder draws it. The shell lays out for that width, not the window's, leaves the page's <main> and skip links to the host, and titles the main column with an h2. Where a page has no matchMedia, no media query matches.
  • gap and rail: 32 and 67 by default, 12 and 76 for cards. The sums use them, so they must match --genai-shell-gap and the rail's button and padding. In the cards frame the breakpoints count the margins and each rail's gap too.
  • color: Designsystemet's data-color for the shell, set only when given.
  • ShellTools, for the secondary panel's children: its tools (id, label, content), value and onChange, headingLevel, and pinIn, the panel's pinned region to draw the switcher in, so it stays while the tool scrolls. One tool gets a heading, two get tabs and three or more a dropdown, the design's tool rule (board 3, kunnskapsassistenten#168). The tabs follow the tabs pattern: one tab stop, the arrows choose, Home and End go to the ends. The dropdown is Designsystemet's Select. Every tool stays mounted while hidden. A tool's icon stands beside its name in the tinted switcher. variant: tinted is the dev boards' switcher (dev-verktoy-2 and -3): two tools as joined buttons on the host family's tinted ground, the chosen one plain with a border, as Designsystemet's ToggleGroup; three or more as a tinted button as wide as the panel, «Fremgangsmåte, velg verktøy», that opens Designsystemet's Dropdown with the tools, the one shown marked aria-current. In the cards frame the tools card's parts stand 12 apart, with no line under the switcher, and its folded button has a ground of its own.
  • ShellProfile, for the primary panel's pinned foot and, with compact, its railFoot: the reader as one full-width tertiary, neutral button with an accent avatar (xs, initials) and the name, cut with «…», the whole name in aria-label and title; on a rail, the avatar alone. It opens the profile menu above itself, Designsystemet's Dropdown: the avatar, the name and the organisation at the top; then settings («Innstillinger»), feedback («Gi tilbakemelding»), changelog («Endringslogg», with a dot when news), about («Om tjenesten») and contact («Kontakt {to}»), with Aksel's icons; then extra, the host's own entries in its order, each with an id, its own label in the reader's language and an optional icon, hidden from screen readers; a line; and signOut («Logg ut», a link, with onSignOut first). Each entry is an href, an onSelect or both; one the host does not give, or one with neither, is not drawn. There is no cog. Nothing is drawn without a name.

What is built

  • Three columns on one row, named after their place, never a side: the primary panel is a nav, the secondary one an aside, each named by the app after what is in it, so a panel that moves keeps a true name (decided 8 and 11 September). The main column scrolls as a whole and centres an 800 px reading width, so the wheel works in its margins.
  • Widths: the primary panel 400 (328 inside), the secondary panel 432 with a floor of 336, and the main column 640 to 800. A panel has no ceiling: it may grow until the main column is at 640 (kunnskapsassistenten#122). When a window is too narrow for what is open, the secondary panel gives first, then the primary one (14 September).
  • Folding: «Skjul …» folds a panel to a 67 px rail with an icon button, «Vis …», which carries aria-expanded, aria-controls and a tooltip with the same words. On the open primary panel the button shows the word «Skjul». No count on the rail (kunnskapsassistenten#87).
  • Resizing (kunnskapsassistenten#80, #81): drag the edge, with a mouse, a pen or a finger. A click on the edge makes the panel as wide as the window allows and the next click puts it back, so no drag is needed (WCAG 2.5.7). The edge is a separator that takes the keyboard: the arrows move it 16 px, Shift and an arrow 64, Home and End go to the narrowest and the widest, and Enter goes back to the design's width. It reports the width in pixels. Dragged past half its floor, a panel folds instead of becoming unreadable, and dragging back 16 px past the line opens it again. Where floor and ceiling are the same, as at 1440 with both panels open, there is no edge to drag, so none is drawn (17 September).
  • One panel below 1440: where both do not fit, opening one folds the other, and a window that shrinks past 1440 folds the secondary panel. Growing back opens it again, unless the reader opened or folded a panel in the meantime (12 November; until then growing back opened nothing).
  • Drawers below 1139: both panels stand as rails, and an open panel is Designsystemet's modal Dialog over the main column, in from its own edge, at the design's width and never wider than the window: focus is trapped, Escape closes it, focus goes back, and it has «Lukk …». Entering drawer mode folds both, so nothing opens over the answer by itself (17 September); leaving it opens again what it folded, where only one fits the one that does not yield (12 November).
  • The top row below 774 (kunnskapsassistenten#120): two rails and a main column under its floor do not fit, 67 + 640 + 67, so the rails become one row above the main column, which gets the whole width. The row holds the two icon buttons, the primary panel's first and the secondary panel's last, each on the text edge from its own side of the window, 16 px on a phone: in the row a rail is as wide as its button and that edge on each side. They open the same drawers, which close with Escape and give the focus back to the button. Grid placement, not other markup, so what is in the panels keeps its state and the tab order stays primary button, main column, secondary button.
  • Focus: when a panel folds with the focus in it, the focus goes to the panel's button, never to <body>. A region that scrolls with nothing in it to reach becomes a tab stop of its own. Pinned heads set scroll padding, so nothing with focus hides under them (WCAG 2.4.11).
  • The height budget (21 September): the reading window is the main column less its pinned head and the compose field. The shell keeps kunnskapsassistenten's paddings, 32 above the column and around the panels, 12 under a pinned head, so the budget is the same; the panel's controls share one row.
  • Remembered: which panels are folded, the widths the reader moved (and only those, so a reset is forgotten), and whether the reader shut a panel, so new content does not open it again until they ask for it.
  • Data in, events out. The shell never stores or fetches anything.

Branding

The block sits in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. The chrome is pinned to the neutral family on purpose, as kunnskapsassistenten's shell: the page, the panels, the cards, the rails, the top row, the drawer, the borders and lines, the titles and the profile menu's top stay grey whatever data-color the app sets, because accent belongs to the content and to what can be pressed. The avatars and the dot on «Endringslogg» are accent, as the design draws them, by data-color in the markup. The separator's line takes the accent role without a family. The widths are not tokens: the shell sets them from its own sums.

What we have learned

  • Made from kunnskapsassistenten's layout/: Shell, PanelSeparator, PanelHead, ViewHead, LayoutProvider's rules, and viewModel's and resize's sums, with the same words, sizes, breakpoints and spacing. Their tests are ported, including the sweep of every width from 280 to 2000 px that checks nothing scrolls sideways.
  • The foot is a slot. What kunnskapsassistenten puts in it has blocks of its own: the links are Settings' SettingsLinks, and who is signed in is Threads' SignedIn.
  • What the app keeps: kunnskapsassistenten stores the layout in the browser under ka.layout.v1, and decides itself when the sources panel is offered (an answer with sources) or opened (a footnote).
  • Which content sits in which panel is the app's. Kunnskapsassistenten puts threads and the filter in the primary panel, as two views with «Filtrer dokumenter» and «← Tråder» between them, and tries the filter over the sources behind the experiment filters-right-panel (kunnskapsassistenten#84). Both are the app's content, not the shell's.
  • Decisions behind it: the vision of 8 September (VS Code's structure, resizable columns, named after their role), 14 September (one panel below 1440, the sources give way to 336), 15 September (the 67 px rail; a pointer path without dragging), 17 September (drawers below 1139; no edge where there is nothing to drag), 21 September (the height budget: the panel's controls on one row, «Skjul» as an icon button), 9 November (the top row below 774 is the standard; kunnskapsassistenten has it behind the experiment mobile-top-row), and the issues above.

Open questions

  • Moving a view to the other panel: kunnskapsassistenten's model can, but it has no control for it. The vision says the reader should choose, later.
  • kunnskapsassistenten#168 asks the secondary panel to switch between tools with tabs for two and a menu for three or more. Kunnskapsassistenten has no such switch, so it is not built; an app can put one in the panel's head.
  • The app builder (genki#210) would place blocks in the shell's panels.
