Conversation

BuildingPart of ChatMain area

The conversation in the chat: the reader's questions, the answers and the questions back, in order, with when each answer came, the thread while it is read, and search in an answer.

UI name
«Samtalen»
Sits in
Main area, over the question field
Code
Conversation and AnswerTime in @genai/ui-blocks. Each answer is Answer.
Issue
kunnskapsassistenten#174
Canvas
00-Chat (FM-0, FM-0b, FM-0c, FM-0d, FM-2b)

Use it when

  • An assistant answers questions in a thread, one turn after another.

Variants

  • Question: the reader's words in a tinted box at the end of the line, with the documents it was asked with under them.
  • Answer: the Answer block, with the line about what the question was narrowed to over it, and when it came at the end of the row under it.
  • Question back («Trenger avklaring»): a finished turn, not a failure. No time and no row; the answers the service offers are choices when the app takes the reply.
  • Thread being read: a question and an answer in outline, said once in words through a live region. What is already on screen wins over the outline.
  • Search in an answer («Søk i svaret», when the host asks for it): one strip pinned over the conversation, one answer at a time, with the count as a live region, steps that stop at the ends, and Escape back to the button.

The block draws and raises events. The app holds the thread, sends, and fills two slots: over each answer (for Approach) and the row under it (for the answer actions, which take the time as their end).

Props

  • turns: questions, answers and questions back, in order. An answer carries what Answer takes, its failure, its createdAt and its scope. The scope is a line of the app's, or the facts, {narrowedTo, corpus}, which the block words as Kunnskapsassistenten does: «Avgrenset til: Årsrapport · 2023», «…, fra Kudos», or «Hentet fra Kudos». Keep it with the turn.
  • follow (on by default) keeps the column at its end while an answer comes, if the reader is within 80 px of it, and lets go when they scroll up. scrollContainer is the column, or the nearest ancestor that scrolls. onAtEndChange says when the reader leaves or reaches the end, for the composer's «Bla til bunnen».
  • slowAfter (30 seconds): an answer on its way with no progress that long gets the line «Dette tar lengre tid enn vanlig.», said once. After stalledAfter (5 minutes), onStalled lets the app stop it. Progress is a new turn object.
  • loading: the thread is being read.
  • onOpenCitation, onOpenDocument, onRegenerate and onReply, each with the turn it came from. target names an answer and a footnote to scroll to and focus.
  • header(turn, question) over an answer or a question back, and actions(turn, {end, search}) under a finished answer. Without actions, the row holds the time alone.
  • search, citationMode, sourcesUsed, closingQuestion, assistantName, href, citationId and headingLevel.
  • questionColor, answerColor and color set Designsystemet's data-color, each only when given. Kunnskapsassistenten's look is accent questions and neutral answers.

Related

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Four are pinned to warning on purpose, as Kunnskapsassistenten marks search matches: the answer spends accent on its footnotes. The question back's tag is info. Four values are not tokens: the question's width, the search field's width and widest width, and the strip's stacking order.

Parity with Kunnskapsassistenten

Read against MessageList, AnswerMessage, AnswerTime, Clarification, ThreadLoading and its tests on kunnskapsassistenten's main, and AnswerSearch as it was before kunnskapsassistenten#209 took it out of the answer's row.

  • Ported, with tests: the ordered list and «Du skrev:»; the question's box, size and attachments line; the scope line over an answer and a question back, with its words for a screen reader; one time per answer, finished or stopped, never on a question, a question back or an answer still written, outside every button, and none for a time that cannot be read; the question back's info tag, its [N] as text and no row; the outline while a thread is read, said through a region already in the page; search with its count, steps, Escape and focus.
  • Kept: the time reads as Kunnskapsassistenten writes it, «14:32», and not in genki's «14.32»: the blocks follow Kunnskapsassistenten here (decided 3 November 2026). Following the answer, the slow notice and the scope's words are Kunnskapsassistenten's too.
  • Changed: search counts answers only in «Søk i svar 2 av 3», where Kunnskapsassistenten counted questions back too. Search is off unless the host asks, as Kunnskapsassistenten has none since #209. A question back draws the choices the contract's clarification.options carries, which Kunnskapsassistenten never got.
  • The app's, not the block's: «Fremgangsmåte» and the thinking panel go in the header slot; the failed turn's card speaks for its failure, where Kunnskapsassistenten had an alert under the conversation; the announcements when an answer or a question back arrives stay with the app's stream.

What we have learned

  • On the 37 answers genki-test gave on 6 November 2026, each after its question, the conversation draws 237 footnotes, as many as the answer block draws alone, and the scan finds 0 leftovers from the source format in the 74 turns.
  • Search looks in the words the answer is drawn with, through the same walk as the drawing: 139 searches in those 37 answers marked 253 matches, each on the characters searched for. A footnote's number is never a match.

Open questions

  • Should search come back to the answer's row? Kunnskapsassistenten took it out in #209; the block keeps it behind search.
  • The fixture holds no question back from genki-test, so that state is tested on written examples only.
