App builder catalogue

BuildingData, no UIThe builder only

The blocks the app builder offers, at the canvas's grain: where each may stand, in what order, what it needs from the service, and the options a service owner may set. With them, the eight templates, the rules a layout must keep, and the service facts the needs are checked against. It draws nothing: the builder page does.

Use it when

  • A builder offers blocks to turn on, place and set, as the canvas's board 3.
  • A host checks a stored layout before it draws it (V2-0006).
  • A host needs to know which blocks a service can back.

What it holds

  • The blocks, blockCatalogue: nine, each made of the package's smaller blocks (its members). Seven are built; Prosjekter and Notater are marked «kommer».
  • The templates, templates and fromTemplate: the canvas's eight, in its order.
  • The layout, AssistantLayout: the blocks per zone, each with only the options that differ from the default. What the builder edits, and what a frontend definition could store.
  • The facts, factsFromModels: what a service can back, read from its /v1/models row.
  • The words, in the catalogue's builder namespace: every block's name and help, every option, template and zone, and a reason for every need, in bokmål and English.

The secondary panel's tools

toolMode is the shell's rule, re-exported here: one tool gives a heading, two give tabs, three or more a dropdown. The builder writes it on the right zone, «To verktøy – faner».

The blocks

The zones are the shell's: primary «Venstre panel», main «Innhold» and secondary «Høyre panel». A block's first zone is where it stands when it is turned on. A lower order comes first in the zone, so the order in a zone is the catalogue's, whatever order the blocks were turned on in.

The need words

From the kits file's words. Each has a reason the builder shows beside a block it cannot offer, from needKey, and every one is used by a block or an option.

  • service:* is read from the service's row: chat «Tjenesten svarer ikke i en chat.», datasets|documents, documents, filter-fields, steps and variants.
  • channel:* is a channel the host gives the client: conversations, facets and feedback.
  • component:sources|document-view is about the layout: Kilder or Dokumentvisning must be placed too.
  • genki:projects is something genki does not have yet.

The templates

The canvas's eight, in its order. A template lists the blocks the design puts in each zone, also those not built: the builder shows them marked «kommer», and fromTemplate leaves them out, with the blocks the service cannot back. Choosing one replaces the layout. «Kunnskapsassistenten» has Innstillinger and no Dokumentvisning, unlike the canvas, so it draws the same app as the UI blocks preview.

The layout and its rules

  • AssistantLayout is { version: 1, template?, zones }, with each zone a list of { block, settings? }. defaultLayout has every built block in its default zone. normalised sorts each zone into the catalogue's order and drops what the catalogue does not hold. isAssistantLayout checks the shape only.
  • BuilderState adds the builder's screen state: chosen, where a movable block goes when it is turned on again, and held, the blocks a change of service turned off.
  • turnOn, turnOff, moveTo and placeIn keep a block in one zone at a time, and refuse a zone it may not stand in. withSetting keeps only what differs from the default; settingsOf fills the defaults in.
  • fitService fits a layout to another service: it turns off and holds the blocks the service cannot back, and puts back, where they stood and as they were set, those an earlier service turned off once one can back them. A block turned off by hand stays off.
  • optionInForce: an option is in force when it is on, its blocks are placed and its needs are met. Fotnoter is off while both Kilder and Dokumentvisning are, whatever the switch says.
  • layoutProblems lists every rule a layout breaks, by name: unknown-block, zone-not-allowed, placed-twice, not-built, missing-partner and needs-unmet. An option left at its default asks nothing.
  • settingsSchema gives a block's options as a JSON Schema (2020-12), with ADR 0030's x-label and x-widget.

In the portal builder's words

  • @genai/ui-blocks/panels speaks the words of the portal's builder (digdir/genki#210) over the same rules: the panels left, main and right are the zones primary, main and secondary, and procedure and documentView are approach and document-view.
  • placeOf, isOn, turnOn, turnOff, moveTo, placeIn, blocksAllowedIn, blocksIn, blockPanels and panelOrder take and give its layout, { placed, chosen }, and run the rules above.
  • toBuilderState and fromBuilderState convert between the two shapes. Its shape has no room for a block's options, the template or the held blocks; given the state it came from, toBuilderState keeps them.
  • Its templates are held there in the catalogue's words until one list is chosen, and are built as fromTemplate builds the canvas's: without the blocks that are not built. Four are the same as the canvas's (canvasTemplateOf).

The service facts

  • factsFromModels(rows, service, given) reads the service's rows in /v1/models: _chat, the _datasets with their multiselect fields, _documents, and the _pickable variants, of the service or of a family the host names. servicesIn lists each service once.
  • The channels come from the host. Whether a service reports steps is not in the row: until an answer says it brought none, Fremgangsmåte is offered.
  • offerOf and optionOfferOf say whether the builder may turn a block or an option on, whether it is coming, and the needs that are not met, for the reason beside it. needMet answers one need.

What we have learned

  • The canvas works at a coarser grain than the package: its «Chat» is eleven of our blocks, and their choices are Chat's options.
  • Fotnoter is an option of Chat that needs Kilder or Dokumentvisning, not a block of its own: V2-0010 pairs every footnote with an excerpt, so a footnote never stands where neither shows it.
  • A block the service cannot back is shown off and disabled, with the reason. Turning it off for good on a change of service lost the reader's layout; holding it and putting it back does not.
  • No block imports the catalogue, and a test keeps it so: a host that draws one block takes no builder along.

Open questions

  • Where a layout is stored. V2-0006 proposes a frontend definition in genki; until then the builder keeps it in the browser.
  • The names of the builder's two views, «Oppsett» and «Prøv assistenten», are the canvas's, which marks them as open.
  • Prosjekter needs genki to store projects; Notater is not built.
