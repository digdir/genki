Use it when

  • A thread is new and empty: the greeting over the question field, and the service's suggested questions under it, when it has some.

Don't use it when

  • The thread has turns. The conversation stands there instead.
  • Something is missing or failed. That is States: the welcome is a component of its own, not the shared empty state.

Variants

  • With a name: «Hei, Kari 👋» and «Hva lurer du på?», one heading on two lines.
  • Without a name: «Hei 👋» and «Hva lurer du på?».
  • With suggestions: «Forslag», and one row per question, each a tertiary button with a document icon.
  • Without suggestions: nothing, not even the heading «Forslag».

Props

  • Welcome: name, the reader's first name, if known; without it the greeting is «Hei». headingLevel, 2 by default, under the page's own heading.
  • Suggestions: suggestions, the questions, and onPick with the one picked. The host fills the field with it and puts the caret there; the block sends nothing. headingLevel for «Forslag», 3 by default, under the greeting.
  • Both take color, Designsystemet's data-color for the block, neutral unless given, and className for its root, beside its own.
  • The words are the block's own catalogue, in bokmål and English: «Hei, {name}», «Hei», «Hva lurer du på?» and «Forslag». The suggestions are the host's, in its words.

Where it goes

  • In the UI assembly, the greeting stands over the question field in a new thread, and the suggestions under it, from the profile's suggestions when the layout's chat has suggestions on. A pick fills the field and moves focus there.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The welcome sits in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. Both parts are neutral unless the host passes color, and each suggestion is pinned to neutral in the markup, as kunnskapsassistenten draws them: a list to read, not a row of buttons.

What is built

  • The greeting is one heading over two lines, so the page's outline gets one entry, not a second with nothing under it. The wave is hidden from screen readers.
  • The suggestions are a heading and a plain list of buttons, no landmark. Each question wraps, starts at the line's start, and is one step quieter than the field's text.
  • A pick calls onPick, and nothing else: filling the field and moving focus there are the host's.
  • The air over «Forslag» halves where the block is under 500 px wide.

Parity with Kunnskapsassistenten

Read against views/chat/Welcome and Kickstarters in kunnskapsassistenten, their tests and chat.css, when the blocks were ported (genki#406). The Chat page has the measurement.

  • Ported, with tests: the greeting as one heading, with and without a name; the suggestions, which fill the field and do not send.
  • The same, measured: beside kunnskapsassistenten's start page and one live conversation, at 1440 px under its theme, all 61 sizes, gaps, offsets and colours compared for the chat's blocks were the same.
  • Made data: the suggestions, which kunnskapsassistenten keeps in code per collection (kickstartersFor).
  • Different on purpose: the air over «Forslag» steps at the block's own width instead of the window's.
  • Decided: a service without suggestions of its own gets none, not even the heading, since nothing is shown without data. Kunnskapsassistenten's suggestions for any collection stay in that app.

What we have learned

  • A second heading for «Hva lurer du på?» would be an outline entry with nothing under it, so the greeting is one heading.
  • The suggestions are long questions, so they are rows, not chips.

Open questions

  • Where does a service set its suggestions: in its settings in genki, or in the frontend? The UI assembly takes them from the host's profile today.
  • The canvas greets with «Hva vil du finne ut?», kunnskapsassistenten with «Hva lurer du på?». The block keeps kunnskapsassistenten's wording until this is decided.
