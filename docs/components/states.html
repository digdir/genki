What a block shows when it has nothing to show, when something failed, and when what was asked for does not exist. Three small components the other blocks use.

Use it when

  • Empty: a block has nothing to show yet, such as no threads or no sources before the first question. A normal state, not an error.
  • Error: something the person asked for failed, and they need to know. It can offer «Prøv igjen».
  • Not found: an address or a thread that does not exist. The page is this state, with «Gå til forsiden» as the way out.
  • Crash: around the whole app, outside its router, so an error React cannot recover from gives a page with «Last inn på nytt», not a white screen.

Don't use it when

  • Something is loading. That is a skeleton, and a polite live region.
  • To fill an empty panel the service does not have. A block that is not there is not shown.

Variants

  • Empty in a panel (level 3, the default): a small heading under the panel's own.
  • Empty as a page (level 2): a large heading, where the page's title would have stood. Not found is always this.

Props

  • EmptyState: title, description, level (2, 3 or 4; it sets the size too) and, as children, the action that resolves it.
  • ErrorState: title, message and onRetry. focusAfterRetry says where focus goes when a retry clears the error; retryRef hands the app the retry button. headingLevel places the heading in the page's outline, 3 by default.
  • ErrorState with a kind, for an answer that failed: one of errorKinds, which gives the heading, what happened and the advice. message then replaces only what happened, and advice the advice, which names no service's own controls, for a host that names its own. «Prøv igjen» is offered only where it can help (isRetryable). assistantName goes in the sentences that need a subject. errorKindFromStatus reads an HTTP status for only what it establishes; the platform's own error codes are the app's to map.
  • useErrorText: the one set of texts for the error kinds, for any block that says why something failed, such as an answer's card. It returns a function that takes a kind and assistantName and gives title, what and advice in the reader's language.
  • NotFoundState: homeHref, and onGoHome for an app with its own router. title and description default to «Siden finnes ikke».
  • CrashBoundary: the app as children, onReload for «Last inn på nytt» (a reload of the page by default), and onError with what was thrown, for the app's own log.

The texts are the block's that uses them, from its own catalogue. The states have a catalogue of their own for «Prøv igjen», «Gå til forsiden», the page that does not exist, the crash page and the error kinds.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The states sit in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follow data-color-scheme and data-size, and read every token through their own variable, with the token as the fallback. Two are pinned to neutral on purpose: an empty state's text is neutral in any family, while the action beside it follows the host's, and the crash page's ground is neutral, as kunnskapsassistenten draws it. The error is Designsystemet's Alert in danger; set its --dsc-alert-* variables to change it. The crash page's width is a reading measure in characters, since no token is a line length.

What is built

  • The empty state is a heading and a line, never an alert: nothing missing is an error. Its size follows its level, so the two cannot disagree: small in a panel, large as a page.
  • The error is an alert region that stays in the page while there is no error, since role="alert" announces only what appears inside a region already there. Empty, it is out of the flow but still focusable.
  • When «Prøv igjen» clears the error, focus goes to focusAfterRetry, or to the alert region, so it never drops to the page. Focus the app moved on purpose, and an error that clears on its own, leave focus alone.
  • Not found is an empty state at level 2, not an alert: the page is this state, and an alert would announce what the person is about to read anyway. Its link is a real link; with onGoHome, a plain click goes through the app's router, and a click with a modifier key opens a new tab as usual.
  • The error kinds are kunnskapsassistenten's, with its words: the model, the search, a timeout, a refused key, too many questions, a question too long, a refused filter, a thread that is gone, and the rest. Each needs something different of the reader. A stopped answer and a search with no hits are not errors and have no kind.
  • The crash page is the page: its own main and a level 1 heading, which takes the focus left on <body>. The error text is behind a Details, to copy into a message. Only NotFoundError, a node moved from under React, gets a line on extensions and translation, its one known cause.

Parity with Kunnskapsassistenten

Drawn side by side on 13 November 2026: kunnskapsassistenten's components/EmptyState, ErrorState and NotFoundState with their rules in styles/global.css, from its main at ef434b7a, beside this package's states at 26f11892. Both ran under kunnskapsassistenten's theme and Designsystemet 1.23.0, in Chrome at 1440 px, in five cases: empty in a panel with an action, empty as a page, an error with a heading and «Prøv igjen», the alert region with no error, and not found.

  • The same: all 17 elements, each compared with its pair: 22 computed properties, among them colour, background, font, size, weight, line height, margins, padding, gap, border and radius; the width and height of its box; and its role, tabindex, link, data-color, data-size and text. 539 values, and none differs. The accessibility trees are the same too: the heading levels, the alert region with and without its error, the button and the link.
  • Ported, with tests: the error is announced when it appears, from a region already in the page, and the empty state never; focus after a retry goes to the given target or the region, and is left alone when the app moved it or the error cleared on its own; not found is a level 2 empty state, not an alert.
  • Changed on purpose: «Prøv igjen», «Gå til forsiden» and the texts of a page that does not exist come from the states' catalogue, in bokmål and English, where kunnskapsassistenten took retryLabel and wrote the rest in place. Not found takes its way out as data, homeHref and onGoHome, instead of kunnskapsassistenten's router link.
  • Decided: the error's heading follows where it sits, through headingLevel, as the empty state's follows level. Its default, 3, is the level kunnskapsassistenten always gave it.
  • Added: kind, with the error texts of kunnskapsassistenten's views/chat/errorText.ts, and CrashBoundary.

What we have learned

  • Drawn small, a not-found page was the smallest thing on a page it was the whole of. As a page it takes the size the page's title would have had.
  • A retry button that removes itself must hand focus on, or the next Tab starts over at the skip link.
  • One alert region per block, not one per error: two voices in no order are harder to follow than one.
