UI assembly

BuildingNot a block

Puts the blocks together from a layout and wires them to a genki service. The blocks stay data in, events out; the assembly holds the state they share, links their events and makes the calls.

Code
Assistant, useAssistant and AssistantView in packages/ui-assembly
Drawn by
The app builder's «Prøv assistenten», and the UI blocks preview, which is kunnskapsassistenten built from blocks
Made from
The UI blocks preview's own wiring, and kunnskapsassistenten's views

Use it when

  • An app is an assistant a layout describes: the zones and options the builder's catalogue allows, live against one genki service.

Don't use it when

  • An app draws its own arrangement. The blocks are the parts; take them from @genai/ui-blocks and wire them yourself.

What happens when a partner is off

  • Without Tråder: one thread per visit, and no thread in the address.
  • Without Filter: the question goes to the service's collection, narrowed by nothing. Without the corpus choice: the service's own collection. A service with no collection, a plain chat, is asked with none.
  • Without Kilder: a footnote opens Dokumentvisning. Without both: no footnotes, since every footnote is paired with an excerpt.
  • Without Fremgangsmåte: no steps are asked for.
  • Without Innstillinger: the setup's display level, and the page is the chat everywhere.
  • With one variant: no picker.

Where the blocks go

  • The primary panel holds Tråder and Filter as two views, and Innstillinger in its foot. The main column holds Chat, or the filter strip with «Endre» and Fremgangsmåte over it. The secondary panel's tools are offered by how many there are: a heading, tabs or a dropdown.
  • Fremgangsmåte is the card over each answer in the main column, the tool in the secondary panel, or both, as the layout places it. The tool is the detailed one: each step's time as genki reports it for the step's tool calls, the filter the answer read from with its values, the sources used, how long it took, and the step frames at the detailed level. Beside the tool, the card keeps only the steps, the totals, «filteret er endret» and, in the next look, how many documents the answer read from.
  • Dokumentvisning is a tool in the secondary panel, or takes the conversation's place in the main column while a document is open.
  • Chat in the secondary panel opens it from the start, as a drawer on a phone. Every way back to the answer shows Chat's tool again.
  • The next look, a layout from the template knowledge-assistant-next: the shell's cards frame, with no top bar. Every panel is titled alike, «Tråder» and «Verktøy», and the primary panel lands on «Tråder». The thread's title is the page's h1 at the top of the main card, where the conversation no longer names it, and the hidden h1 with the assistant's name is gone. With a reader in the profile, the reader stands pinned at the primary panel's foot, the shell's ShellProfile, in place of «Innlogget som» and the links: its menu has «Innstillinger», the profile's menu pages and «Logg ut» when the profile gives a sign-out. Without a reader, the links stay. The docked field stands on the card's ground. Every other template draws what it drew before.
  • The next look's folded panels (dev-navbar, dev-verktoy-4): the threads' panel folds to its button and «Ny tråd» as a plus, a link to a new thread, with no avatar; the tools' panel folds to its button alone, with no tool icons. The tools' icons stand in the switcher: Fremgangsmåte's robot and Kilder's FileSearchIcon.
  • The next look's answer (dev-kilder-hoyre): no card and no name over it (the Conversation block's answerLabel off), and footnote marks 16 px tall, --genai-answer-footnote-size set to --ds-size-4 on the chat column.
  • The next look's thread list is the Threads block's variant="nav" with no search: «Ny tråd» first, under «Tråder», and no «Filtrer dokumenter», whose filter has a panel of its own. «Ny tråd» keeps the panel on «Tråder».
  • The next look's «Kilder» folds the documents the answer read but cites nowhere under «Andre kilder som ble lest (N)», the Sources block's foldUncited. The cited ones stand as before.
  • The next look's filter (dev-filter-panel): its own panel in the threads' card, the shell's companion, titled «Filter» with «Gjelder neste spørsmål», in the filter's panel variant, with «Dokumentene som blir med» counting what the next question reads from. Each field's row is named by its profile heading. The filter belongs to the thread: the filter of each thread's last question asked here is kept in the storage (threadFilters), and opening the thread brings it back; genki keeps no filter with a turn, so a thread asked elsewhere leaves the filter as it is. An answer asked here says its filter inside «Fremgangsmåte», with «· filteret er endret» when it differs from the answer before. No line under the field says what the next question reads from: the filter's panel does. The counts are exact, from the facets and the profile's typeField.
  • A narrower filter, the trial compact-filter, when the profile offers it and the reader turns it on: the next look's filter panel beside the threads is 230 px wide, its ticks in one column, and the threads take the rest of the card. Resizing the card changes the threads' width only; the card goes no narrower than the threads' 220 beside the filter. In a drawer the filter stands under the threads at the drawer's width, its ticks in one column. Off, the filter is as before: 440 of the card's 660, which goes no narrower.
  • Under the next look's answer: no follow-up questions over the field, whatever the profile's followUps and Chat's option say, and «Kopier lenke til tråden» with no note that the link works only here: the answer actions' linkOnlyHere is off. The answer's time stays at the row's end.

Props

  • api: the calls, from createGenkiApi({ baseUrl, tenant, profile, language, headers }), or a fake in tests. A host reaches genki through a proxy that adds the key, or passes headers, a callback that returns its credentials, such as { authorization: 'Bearer …' } with the person's token. It is called before every request, the question's stream included, so a renewed token is used; Accept and Content-Type stay the assembly's own. A 401 fails the call with that status, as HttpError or @genai/client's ApiError, which the assembly shows as access; a question stops at its new thread.
  • layout: the builder's layout, zones and options.
  • service and tenant; model, the variant to start from.
  • profile: what genki does not say. The assistant's name, suggestions, follow-ups, the collections' words and fields, the family of services, the trials and who is signed in. For the next look, also the reader's organisation and the profile menu's menu pages: feedback, changelog (with news for its dot), about and contact (with to), each an href or an onSelect, and extra, the host's own entries in its own words, such as a way back to an older client, before «Logg ut». A page not given is not drawn. signOut is the host's sign-out, an href and an optional onSignOut: «Logg ut» first forgets the reader's choices kept in this browser, the threads' filters included, then calls it and goes there. Without it, no «Logg ut» is drawn, in either look.
  • now: the clock, for the year a year field runs up to; tests give a fixed one.
  • threadInUrl: the thread as ?t=, opened only when the id looks like one genki makes, «Innstillinger» as #innstillinger, and the tab's title. With paths, a path per page. allThreads lists every thread under the key.
  • storage: where the reader's choices are kept, such as browserStorage('preview.').
  • onFeedback, onRename and onDelete: without them, none is offered. onFeedback gets the answer's answerId, the rating, reasons and comment, on the click on a thumb and again in full with a comment, and runId: genki's id for the run that gave the answer, which POST /api/feedback takes as task. It comes from the stream's genai-task header, or from task on a stored answer, so a proxy passes that header on; without it, runId is left out and the host decides. It comes from the page, so a host whose proxy uses one shared key sends feedback only on runs it made for that reader. onNotice, onThreadChange and onStepsReported tell the host what happened.
  • colorScheme and onColorSchemeChange: the host keeps the scheme. mainId: the target of «Hopp til hovedinnhold».
  • frameWidth: the assistant is drawn in a host's preview of that width, on the host's page, so it draws no h1 of its own and passes the width to the shell.

For a host that only tries it

A host that tries the assistant on real threads, such as a preview, keeps three things beside it. They are exported with it, so no host writes its own.

  • useNotice(): [say, view]. say goes to onNotice, and view is drawn once beside the assistant, a polite live region that shows each notice for seven seconds.
  • useStoredColorScheme(key): [scheme, setScheme] for colorScheme and onColorSchemeChange, kept on <html data-color-scheme> and in localStorage under key, and followed when another tab changes it.
  • useLogOnly(say, log): onFeedback, onRename and onDelete that only log and say what they would have done, so a real thread is never changed. «Sender …» shows for a moment, as a real request would.

Branding

The assembly's own sheet, @genai/ui-assembly/styles.css, arranges the blocks and styles only its own classes. It sits in @layer genai.components and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. The reading width is the one value that is not a token: kunnskapsassistenten's 728 px. The docked field reads the shell's end padding through the shell's variable.

What is built

  • Every word the reader sees is in a catalogue, bokmål with English held to it. The blocks' words are read from theirs: the blocks' names, «Ny tråd», «Avgrenset til».
  • A question asked before genki has said what the service backs is sent once it has. A failed /v1/models is shown, and tried again when a question is sent.
  • A question asked in Dokumentvisning names the document, and searches only that document where the collection declares its number as a filter field.
  • A field the profile marks kind: 'year' offers only years, newest first: from its years.first, 1900 without it, up to its years.last, the year it is in Norway without it (now). Page numbers, parse noise and years far ahead, which genki returns as they are, are left out, but a value the reader has chosen is always kept, so its chip can be removed.
  • A filter field with every value chosen, as after «Velg alle», narrows nothing, so it is left out of what goes to genki and of «Avgrenset til». The facets are asked without a field that has every value in the collection chosen, the question without one that has every value it lists chosen. «Avgrenset til» names the values as the filter does, years in a row as one period.
  • genki's filter takes at most 100 values a field. A field with more chosen and at most 100 left out goes as the values left out, not-in-set: by the collection's values for the facets, by the field's list for the question. That keeps documents with no value in the field, and drops a document that has a value left out among several. With more than 100 on both sides, nothing is sent: the filter says so, and a question fails with «Filteret kan ikke brukes» and why.
  • A facets address longer than 8,000 characters is not sent: Node answers 431 past 16 KB of headers, so the filter says it could not fetch the fields instead.
  • «Kopier svaret» copies what the screen says, through the answer block's answerCopy. «Kopier lenke til tråden» is offered only where the thread is in the address.
  • The page has one h1, the assistant's name, before the rest of the main column, wherever Chat stands.
  • An assistant that goes stops its answer, and reports nothing after.
  • In the next look, the front page gives way to the question: while the field holds text, the greeting and the suggestions are hidden (data-typing on the chat column, visibility: hidden), and they come back when it is emptied. They keep their room, so the field does not move under the caret, and focus stays in the field.
  • Dokumentvisning's hint names what opens a document in the layout: «Vis i dokumentet» in Kilder, or a footnote when Kilder is off.
  • The next look draws a footnote that names a document, the Answer block's documentFootnotes, until genki cites excerpts: one number per cited document, 1, 2, 3 by first citation, leading to the document in «Kilder». «Kopier svaret» copies the same numbers. Answers that cite excerpts keep one number per excerpt.
  • The assembly measures nothing of the shell's. The shell pads a panel's region as tall as its pinned switcher from the moment the panel opens, and Dokumentvisning's jumps stop under the switcher at the height the shell hands its tools, --uib-shell-pinned-block-size.

What we have learned

  • A footnote that leads into a hidden tool goes nowhere: with Chat and Kilder in one panel, the way back has to show Chat's tool before the focus can land.
  • genki's stored answers keep no steps, so Fremgangsmåte has nothing to show for a thread opened later, and says so.

Open questions

  • Should a question asked in a document be marked in the thread as one, as the canvas's «Spørsmål til én kilde» does?
  • How should a field with more than 100 chosen and more than 100 left out be asked for, such as 200 of Kudos's 457 «Virksomhet»? genki's filter takes neither side, so the filter says so and nothing is sent.
  • Dokumentvisning's jumps read the shell's pinned height only because the assembly hands it to them. Should the block read --uib-shell-pinned-block-size itself, as the shell's page offers it to a block that pins its own head?
