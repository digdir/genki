Use it when

  • Users should be able to see and judge what the answer is based on.
  • Each footnote should lead to what it cites, and each excerpt back to where the answer uses it.

Variants

  • By excerpt (the default): only cited excerpts get a number, «Utdrag 1», «Utdrag 2», in the order the answer first cites them. Documents are named by title. Needs footnotes that name their excerpt.
  • By document: nothing is numbered. Each excerpt shown in a cited document links back to the document's uses; a document nothing cites says so.
  • Panel, with a search over the excerpts, and compact, as a section after the answer.

The service sets the mode; a reader can switch when the app passes onCitationModeChange. An excerpt is titled by its section, with its number before it. A closed excerpt shows its first two lines.

Props

  • sources: the contract's sources[].
  • citations: the answer's footnotes in order, each naming the excerpt (chunkId) or document it cites. Without them nothing is numbered.
  • target and onReturn: from a footnote to its excerpt and back. onTargetMissing reports a footnote whose excerpt is not shown.
  • onShowUse and citationHref: an excerpt has a link back to each footnote that cites it, in the order they stand in the answer, as Wikipedia's back-references. Cited once, «Til svaret»; several times, «↩ 1 2 3». Each is a real link, so a reader can tab to or open any one, and each says to a screen reader which footnote it leads to. They replace one control that cycled (decided 6 November 2026).
  • answerIndex, answerCount and onAnswerChange: one answer's sources at a time, «Kilder til svar 2 av 3» with «Forrige» and «Neste», since every answer numbers its excerpts from 1. The app holds which answer is shown and passes its sources.
  • answerStatus and sourcesNotStored: an empty panel says why. Nothing asked yet, stopped before the sources came, failed, waiting on a question back, or sources the store did not keep. streaming shows the loading state.

pairCitations is exported, so the answer numbers its footnotes the same way.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. Seven are pinned to a colour family on purpose: accent for what can be pressed and the excerpt a footnote points at, warning for search hits.

What we have learned

  • A number on an excerpt with no visible use reads as made up. Only what a footnote cites gets a number, and the pairing comes from the answer's footnotes, never a guess.
  • Kudos excerpts are Marker's Markdown from PDFs. The block drops page markers, rules and running heads, and draws tables, links and tags as text; a scan checks what a reader sees for leftovers.
  • Compact rows and the info control with document and chunk ids come from the playground's sources list, digdir/genki#112.
