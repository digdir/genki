Use it when

  • Users should be able to see and judge what the answer is based on.
  • Each footnote should lead to what it cites, and each excerpt back to where the answer uses it.

Variants

  • Footnotes that name excerpts: only cited excerpts get a number, «Utdrag 1», «Utdrag 2», in the order the answer first cites them, and they read in that order from the top: the documents by their lowest number, and in each, its cited excerpts by number. An excerpt nothing cites is unnumbered, last in its document, says «Ikke vist til i svaret», and has no link back. Documents are named by title.
  • Footnotes that name only documents, as long as a service cites documents: nothing is numbered, since footnotes are numbered by excerpt and never by document. A document's passages, which nothing cites alone, are closed behind «Vis utdrag fra dokumentet (n)». A document no footnote names says so. The documents and their passages keep the service's order.
  • Panel, with a search over the excerpts, and compact, as a section after the answer.

Which of the two is read from citations, never chosen. An excerpt is titled by its section, with its number before it. A closed excerpt shows its first two lines.

Props

  • sources: the contract's sources[].
  • citations: the answer's footnotes in order, each naming the excerpt (chunkId) or document it cites. Excerpts are numbered only when every footnote names its excerpt. Without footnotes nothing is numbered.
  • target and onReturn: from a footnote to its excerpt and back. onTargetMissing reports a footnote whose excerpt is not shown.
  • onShowUse and citationHref: the links back to the answer sit where the excerpt is, right beside its title, «Utdrag 1», which is a title and not a link. One small link per footnote that cites the excerpt, 1, 2, 3 in the order they stand in the answer, as Wikipedia's back-references. Each is a real link, so a reader can tab to or open any one. Its name starts with the number seen and says which footnote and which place it leads to: «1: fotnote 2, første sted i svaret». Nothing on a document's head or over the list links back (decided 6 November 2026).
  • answerIndex, answerCount and onAnswerChange: one answer's sources at a time, «Kilder til svar 2 av 3» with «Forrige» and «Neste», since every answer numbers its excerpts from 1. The app holds which answer is shown and passes its sources.
  • answerStatus and sourcesNotStored: an empty panel says why. Nothing asked yet, stopped before the sources came, failed, waiting on a question back, or sources the store did not keep. streaming shows the loading state. loading shows it too, before anything is known. assistantName names who asked back, «KI-assistenten» without it.
  • variant: panel (the default), compact, or tool, the dev boards' «Kilder» tool (dev-kilder-hoyre): the search with «Søk i utdragene» as its placeholder and name, the caption «Utdrag svaret bygger på» naming the list, each excerpt titled by the last step of its heading where it has one, with its links back after it, else «Utdrag N», a document's excerpts in its card even when the footnotes name only documents, and a closed excerpt as two lines of its text, the last fading out. search draws the search over the excerpts, on in the panel. title is the heading, «Kilder», or false for none. headingLevel is each document's heading level, 2 to 5, 3 by default. documentsClosed starts every card closed.
  • foldUncited: the documents the answer read but cites nowhere stand after the cited ones, in a Designsystemet Details, «Andre kilder som ble lest (N)», closed. A search hit or a target in one of them opens it, and the search steps through the hits in the order drawn. Nothing is folded when the answer cites none of them, or without citations.
  • corpus names the collection in the disclaimer and the links, «Les dokumentet på Kudos». disclaimer (on by default) says the text is quoted, not generated; disclaimerId is that sentence's id, which the search field is described by, when the app needs to know it. hasOwnDocuments says the reader's own documents are among the sources, when the ids don't tell.
  • details shows each document's and chunk's ids behind an info control. itemId and excerptId give each card and excerpt an id the app can link to.
  • onSelect: the reader opened an excerpt or followed a document's link. excerptActions and sourceActions are slots for the app's own buttons, on an open excerpt and on a card's head.
  • color and headerColor set Designsystemet's data-color on the block and on the cards' heads, each only when given. className is added to the block's own; children come after the cards.

pairCitations is exported, so the answer numbers its footnotes the same way, and so is readingOrder, so «Kilder brukt i svaret» lists the documents in the order Sources shows them.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. Eight are pinned to a colour family on purpose: accent for what can be pressed and the excerpt a footnote points at, warning for search hits, and neutral for the disclaimer, as kunnskapsassistenten draws it. «Kilder» is a heading one level over the documents', so the cards sit under it in the outline.

What we have learned

  • A number on an excerpt with no visible use reads as made up. Only what a footnote cites gets a number, and the pairing comes from the answer's footnotes, never a guess.
  • Designsystemet's Card makes a click anywhere on a card click the first link in a heading. Here that was an excerpt's link back, so a document card is a plain ds-card, and only a cited excerpt's links lead to the answer.
  • Kudos excerpts are Marker's Markdown from PDFs. The block drops page markers, rules and running heads, and draws tables, links and tags as text; a scan checks what a reader sees for leftovers.
  • Compact rows and the info control with document and chunk ids come from the playground's sources list, digdir/genki#112.
