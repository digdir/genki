Filter

BuildingOptionalPrimary sidebar

Lets the user narrow which documents the assistant reads from, by the fields the dataset declares, such as document type, organisation and year. With it come the collection being searched and the documents behind the answer.

Canvas
00-Filter (FI-A2, FI-4c1 to FI-4c3, FI-P1, FI-P3, FI-P4)

Use it when

  • Users should be able to narrow what the assistant reads from, for example in a large collection such as Kudos.

Don't use it when

  • The assistant should always read from a fixed set. Then the set is chosen at setup, and the filter is left out.

Variants

  • A, the filter belongs to the thread (FI-P1): in the sidebar with the threads. Preferred.
  • B, the filter in the answer: inside Approach (FI-P3).
  • In the thread, at the top of the conversation, or as a tab next to «Kilder» (FI-4c1 to FI-4c3).

Props

  • fields: the dataset's filter fields (/api/datasets/{id}, filters[]) with their facet values (/api/facets), in the order to draw them. Undefined while they load; an empty list says filtering is not available.
  • selection and onChange: what is chosen, per field id. Ask with narrowing(selection, fields), which leaves out a field where every value is ticked.
  • failed and onRetry: the facets could not be fetched. A string in failed says why, in place of «Klarte ikke å hente filtrene.».
  • locked, thread and onNewThread: a thread's lock, and the note when the filter changes in a thread under way. The platform keeps no filter per thread yet, so locked is for a host that keeps one. onNewThread takes a plain click on «Ny tråd»; a click for a new tab follows newThreadHref, the browser's.
  • yearPeriods and compactChips: off by default in the block. The UI assembly always turns both on (they were KA's trials until 13 November 2026). compactChips gathers six or more chosen values in one chip, such as «12 virksomheter»; without it, a field that lists two or more values, all chosen, shows one chip anyway. yearFields: the ids of the fields whose values are years, so a filter in force shows its years as periods, «2020–2022», one chip each, even while the facets are missing; removing a period removes all its years.
  • head and children: the corpus choice over the fields, and the documents under a line after them.
  • pinIn: an element to draw the heading and head in, such as the shell's pinned region from its panel's pinnedRef. They stay in sight while the fields scroll, as kunnskapsassistenten pins them, and keep the filter's live region. null while the element is not there draws no head.
  • documents: the documents behind the answer, over a line before the fields, so they are in sight without scrolling past the fields. KA keeps its list apart from the reader's own documents «so the view can show them above the facets, in sight». KA's filter view today draws them after the fields instead, so the facets keep their full size; a host passes them as children for that order.
  • variant: fields, the default, or panel, the dev boards' filter beside the threads (dev-filter-panel): «Fjern alle filtre» over the fields, and each field a row with its heading and what is chosen, «Alle» when nothing is, or when it lists two or more values and all are chosen («Alle år» for a field of kind year, whose chosen years read as periods such as «2020–2022, 2025»), that opens one at a time to a checkbox per value with its exact count, in two columns, or as many as --genai-filter-checkboxes-columns says: 1 in a narrow panel. A field with more than 12 values opens to its list with search instead. children stand under the rows with no line.
  • FilterRead and FilterNext: the filter where it is not edited. FilterRead is «Leste fra 8 781 dokumenter:» and the chosen values as chips, alike and not clickable, for Approach's scope. FilterNext is the line under the field, «Neste spørsmål leser fra 8 781 dokumenter · Evaluering · 2020–2025 – endres i filteret til venstre», drawn only with a count. Both, and the rows, name values with valueLabels, so they never drift apart; documentCount counts exactly from the facets, never «ca.».
  • DocumentsInScope: «Dokumentene som blir med», a box under the panel's rows. Its summary, «Assistenten leser fra 8 781 av 45 778 dokumenter i Kudos», takes count, total and corpus. The list under it, each document with its type, organisation, year, pages and a tick to leave it out, is drawn only when the host passes documents and onIncludedChange; the platform sends neither yet. In a box too narrow for both, «Skjul» goes under the title.

What we have learned

  • The filter applies to the next question. Earlier answers don't change.
  • When you go back to a thread, the filter shows the choices from the last question in that thread.
  • Numbers are exact, never «ca.». The system knows how many documents there are.
  • Large selections are summarised instead of listed: «Alle 173», «12 av 173 valgt», «172 av 173 · 1 tatt ut». The filter must handle about 45,000 documents.
  • Beyond the filter, users don't choose what the assistant reads. It always reads every source that is included, and the system decides how many it reads closely.
  • «Fjern alle filtre» sits above «Dokumenttype», and the summary has a neutral grey background so it stands apart from the chat.
  • Call it «Filter», not «Avgrensning».

What is built

KA's filter panel, made generic: its look, behaviour and wording, with the fields taken from the dataset instead of a fixed list. The blocks draw and report; the host fetches the facets, keeps the selection and starts threads.

To make the filter and the collection last, the host stores selection and the collection's key from onChange, and passes them back. KA keeps both in the browser and empties them at «Logg ut». A stored value the facets no longer hold is still shown, under «Avgrenset til», so it can be removed.

  • A field per facet: Designsystemet's Suggestion with chips, «Velg alle» and «Tøm», the count of each value in the list, and the state in the description: «Ingen avgrensning», «3 av 12 valgt», «Alle 12 valgt, altså ingen avgrensning». Chosen values come first when the list opens, and nothing moves while it is open. More than 100 chosen in one field, with more than 100 left out, says so, with the way out: genki takes at most 100 values, chosen or left out.
  • One chip for all: when the field lists two or more values and all are chosen, as after «Velg alle», the field shows one chip, «Alle virksomheter», instead of one per value, and the list still ticks them all. Removing it clears the field, and ArrowLeft from the field reaches it. A list of one value never becomes «Alle»: when another field narrows the list to one, each chosen value keeps its own chip.
  • Years as periods (yearPeriods): a field of kind year takes «2021», «2023–2028» or «23-28», and years in a row show as one chip. A period stands for the years in it that the field lists with documents, not every calendar year, so «1800-2015» sends no more values than the collection has years. The list shows a period with those years counted, «1800–2015 (26 år med dokumenter)», since adding the documents per year would count a document with several years more than once; a single year keeps its documents, «2019 (523)». A period with none of them is not offered: «Ingen dokumenter i 1700–1750.»
  • Values no field can show, under «Avgrenset til», removable one by one or with «Tøm»: when the facets fail or come back without a field a stored filter still holds.
  • A locked thread shows what it is limited to, with «Ny tråd». A change in a thread under way shows a note, with «Ny tråd».
  • One live region tells what changed where the combobox says nothing, never twice. Focus never falls to the page when a control removes itself.

Utvalg and documents

  • CorpusChoice, «Dokumentsamling»: a select over the collections a service can search, drawn only with more than one. In the filter's head it speaks through the filter's live region; with resetsFilter it says the filter was reset.
  • CorpusLine: «Dokumenter fra Kudos», with the rest behind «Vis mer»: how many documents, which types, biggest first, and which years, counted from the unnarrowed facets. Without counts, the collection's own description. typePlurals names the types in the plural.
  • Documents: the documents behind the answer on screen, five at first, then «Vis flere dokumenter». A title without a public address is text, and the line under it says why. The reader's own uploads are left to OwnDocuments.
  • OwnDocuments, «Dine dokumenter»: a drop zone and the reader's files with progress, size and why one failed. Without onUpload the zone is switched off and says «Kommer snart». Files go up one at a time; a rejected upload does not stop the next, and the same file can be picked again.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The blocks sit in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follow data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and read every token through their own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. The muted text is pinned to neutral on purpose, so it stays grey whatever family the links and chips beside it follow; a failed upload is pinned to danger. The gap between the fields follows the window's height between two tokens, as kunnskapsassistenten's does.

Open questions

  • How is it shown when the selection changes in the middle of a thread?
  • The canvas uses a technical label in the filter that must not appear in user-facing text. It needs a replacement before the filter is built.
  • The platform's facets carry no label for a field or a value. A host names the fields for now, and a value is shown as it is stored.
