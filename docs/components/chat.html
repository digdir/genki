Use it when

  • Hypothesis: always, as the core of every assistant. To be tested: some assistants may not need a chat at all.

Variants

  • New thread: an empty state, with «Endre» for what the thread reads from right in the question field.
  • With and without side panels: the side panels can be hidden, and the conversation then stays centred at a fixed width.
  • Start page (built): the greeting, the question field and the suggestions as one group, with the disclaimer at the foot of the page. The field is not sticky there.
  • In a conversation (built): the field is sticky at the foot, with a fade over it, «Bla til bunnen» when the reader has scrolled up, and follow-up questions under it.
  • Attachments (built): a paperclip and drop on the field where the service takes files, the paperclip switched off with «Kommer snart» where uploading is coming, and nothing where the host has no attachments.

Props

  • Composer: value, onChange and onSubmit with the question, which every way of sending goes through: Enter, the send button and a follow-up. working holds sending, and with onCancel the send button becomes «Avbryt».
  • attachments: the files as the host tracks them, in items, each with an id, its name, a status (uploading, ready or failed), progress from 0 to 100 while uploading and an error if it failed; with onAdd, onRemove and onRetry, accept for the file picker, and types and maxBytes (shown in megabytes to one decimal) for what it takes, named as in OwnDocuments. The old names, fileTypes and maxMegabytes, still work until hosts have moved. Uploading is the host's; the block draws and refuses.
  • followUps, onScrollToBottom, actions (a slot beside the send button, for the variant picker), assistantName, placeholder, showDisclaimer, shortcut and sticky. Docked with sticky, the block keeps its own height clear under whatever the browser scrolls to in the same column, so the field never hides the focus (WCAG 2.4.11), as kunnskapsassistenten does.
  • note: a line right under the field, docked with it, such as the filter's FilterNext: «Neste spørsmål leser fra 8 781 dokumenter · Evaluering – endres i filteret til venstre» (dev-filter-panel).
  • fieldRef and sendRef: refs to the field and the send button, for a host that moves focus there, such as a skip link. ref is the block's root.
  • Every block here takes a className for its root, beside its own.
  • actions is where the variant picker goes: kunnskapsassistenten decided on 6 October 2026 that the choice of who answers sits in the question field, like the model choice in claude.ai, not in the conversation's head. Its list opens above the field, a long name ends in an ellipsis, and on a narrow field the paperclip drops «Kommer snart» first.
  • The field is controlled, so the draft is the host's: it passes value back after a reload or a sign-in, as kunnskapsassistenten keeps it for the tab.
  • color sets Designsystemet's data-color on the block, and actionColor on the send button and the follow-ups. They are neutral and accent unless given, as a field is in Designsystemet and in kunnskapsassistenten. Welcome, Suggestions and ComposerDisclaimer are neutral too.
  • Welcome: the reader's first name, if known. Suggestions: suggestions and onPick; the host fills the field and puts the caret there, it does not send. Both take headingLevel, 2 for the greeting and 3 for «Forslag» by default.
  • ComposerDisclaimer: the disclaimer on its own, for the foot of the start page.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The question field and the welcome sit in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follow data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and read every token through their own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. Three are pinned to a colour family on purpose: accent for the frame and the hint while a file is dragged over it, and danger for why a file was not attached. The block is neutral and its actions accent unless the host passes color and actionColor. In the markup, four controls are pinned to neutral as kunnskapsassistenten draws them: «Bla til bunnen», the paperclip, a file's «Prøv igjen» and the suggestions. Two values are not tokens, both Kunnskapsassistenten's: the empty field takes a tenth of the screen's height, between two tokens, and grows to at most 274 px.

Parity with Kunnskapsassistenten

Read against views/chat/ in kunnskapsassistenten: Composer, Attachments, ScrollToBottom, useComposerShortcut, Welcome and Kickstarters, their tests and chat.css.

  • Ported, with tests: Enter sends and Shift+Enter breaks the line, never mid-word in an input method; «Avbryt» in words while the answer comes; nothing is sent while a file uploads, and the block says why; the paperclip, the hidden picker and drop on the field; the switched-off paperclip where uploading is coming; chips from uploading to ready or failed, with «Prøv igjen» only where it can help; three announcements per file and none per percent; focus back in the field after remove, retry and cancel; Ctrl+/ and Cmd+/; «Bla til bunnen», which hands the keyboard on and leaves a tap alone; the greeting as one heading; the suggestions, which fill the field and do not send.
  • Moved into the block: focus after cancel and after «Bla til bunnen», and the shortcut, which the chat view did.
  • Made data: the assistant's name, the follow-up questions, the suggestions per dataset, the file types and the size limit.
  • Left to the host: uploading and its progress, the draft kept across a sign-in, whether the conversation is at its end, the glide from the start page to the foot, and the margin that keeps the field from hiding what is scrolled to.
  • Different on purpose: the air over the disclaimer and over «Forslag» steps at the block's own width instead of following the window's.
  • Decided: a service without suggestions of its own gets none, not even the heading, since nothing is shown without data. Kunnskapsassistenten's suggestions for any dataset stay in that app.

What we have learned

  • Kunnskapsassistenten's question field and welcome were close to generic: its name in two sentences was the only thing tied to it.
  • Measured beside Kunnskapsassistenten's start page and one live conversation, at 1440 px under its theme: all 61 sizes, gaps, offsets and colours compared are the same, once the host passes neutral with accent actions. The field measures 88 px empty and at most 274 px at 1440 × 900, and 84 px empty at 390 × 844, as in Kunnskapsassistenten. Its surfaces are neutral while its send button and follow-ups keep the page's accent, which is why the block takes two colour props.
  • The conversation reads in order: the question you asked with its document selection, the answer, the sources used in the answer, and «Spør videre». The input field sits below.
  • Your question has a subtle accent surface and your avatar. The answer has the sparkle icon and no bubble around it.
  • Typical answers are long, about one A4 page. Test the layout with that.
  • What the sources don't say is a finding in itself. It gets its own section after the answer, Ikke funnet i kildene, with actions such as «Let i hele Kudos» and «Bruk i teksten». It must not look like a weak claim.

Open questions

  • Does every assistant need a chat? To be tested.
  • How do we tell “not found” from “not read”, when the assistant reads only a selection closely?
  • The workspace's field has a visible label, a line about personal data and a validation message. Should the question field take them?
