Use it when

  • The reader should know what the assistant reads from: the line, from the collection's name and its facets.
  • The service can search more than one collection, and the reader picks one: the choice.

Don't use it when

  • To narrow the collection. That is the Filter, and the line does not follow it.

Variants

  • One collection: the line only. CorpusChoice draws nothing.
  • Several: the line, and «Dokumentsamling», a select over them.
  • The line with counts: «Dokumenter fra Kudos», and behind «Vis mer» how many documents, which types and which years.
  • The line without counts: the collection's own description behind «Vis mer», or no «Vis mer» when there is none.

Props

  • A collection is a CorpusOption: key, the platform's dataset-config-key; label, what the reader sees, of which a sentence uses the part before a comma; and description, what is in it, in the dataset's own words.
  • CorpusLine: corpus, the collection searched; fields, the filter fields with their facets, unnarrowed; typeField, the field whose values are the document types; and typePlurals, what many of a type are called, «årsrapporter» for «Årsrapport». className for its root.
  • CorpusChoice: options, every collection the service can search, in order; value, the key of the one searched now; onChange with the key chosen; and resetsFilter, when the host empties a filter in force on a switch, so the announcement says so. className for its field.
  • useCorpusText gives the line's two parts, source and detail, for a host that draws it itself. corpusName gives a collection's name for a sentence.

Where it goes

  • In the UI assembly, the choice is the filter's head, over the fields, when the layout's filter has corpus on. The line stands under «Dokumenter» in Documents, or in the strip over the conversation where the layout draws one.
  • What a switch does is the host's. The UI assembly starts a new thread with an empty filter for the other collection, as kunnskapsassistenten does, and passes resetsFilter when a filter is set.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The line and the choice sit in @layer genai.components, after Designsystemet's layers, follow data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and read every token through their own variable, with the token as the fallback. Set one on any ancestor to change that detail only. The line's text is pinned to neutral on purpose, so it stays grey whatever family «Vis mer» beside it follows.

What is built

  • The line counts the documents over one field, as each document has one value there, the same sum the filter counts. A missing count drops that part rather than adding up a partial one.
  • The types come biggest first. Up to five are named, and a sixth is named rather than «med flere» about one; with more, the first five and «med flere». A type without a plural is kept in lower case, not given an invented one.
  • The years are a range, «2015–2024», or one year alone. A part of the sentence whose data is missing is left out.
  • «Vis mer» is a button drawn as a link, with aria-expanded, pointing at the detail, which is always in the page and hidden while closed. Its name says what it is about: «Vis mer om dokumentsamlingen». It stays open across a switch.
  • A collection the line does not know is «standardsamlingen». A label is used up to its first comma.
  • The choice is Designsystemet's Select labelled «Dokumentsamling». Choosing the collection already searched does nothing. A switch is announced, «Dokumentsamling: Kudos», with «Filteret er nullstilt …» when resetsFilter is set: through the filter's live region inside a Filter, or its own beside the field elsewhere.
  • The words are the block's own catalogue, in bokmål and English.

What we have learned

  • The line reads the unnarrowed facets, so ticking a box in the filter cannot shrink what the collection is said to hold.
  • An <output> inside the select's field counts as a second input, so the choice's own live region stands beside the field.

Open questions

  • The canvas labels the choice «Korpus»; kunnskapsassistenten says «Dokumentsamling» to readers, and so does the block. One decision covers this and the filter's label (kunnskapsassistenten#178).
