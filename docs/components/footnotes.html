Footnotes

Use it when

  • Users must be able to check each claim against its source.
  • Each footnote should lead to what it cites, and each excerpt back to where the answer uses it.

Variants

  • By excerpt (the default): a footnote is a small round mark with its excerpt's number, «Utdrag 1», «Utdrag 2», in the order the answer first cites them, whatever number the service wrote. Needs the contract's markers.
  • By document: nothing is numbered. A footnote is a mark with a document in it, named by the document's title.
  • States: a skeleton before the first word and a line while it writes; a stopped answer, whose footnotes say their source never came; a failed one; and a finished answer with no sources, which says so over the text.

The pairing comes from the data, never a guess. A footnote that leads to nothing shown is not drawn, two side by side that lead to one item are one footnote, and a [N] that is no footnote, such as a year, stays text. Each footnote is held on the line of the word before it.

Props

  • text, sources and markers: the answer as the contract sends it. [N] is the marker markers names, or the source with index: N.
  • status: streaming, complete, aborted or failed.
  • onOpenCitation: a footnote was followed, with its index and the item it cites, for Sources' target.
  • target: the footnote to scroll to and focus, where Sources' onShowUse and onReturn lead.
  • onOpenDocument, href, citationId, sourcesUsed, closingQuestion, onRegenerate and the actions slot.

answerCitations gives Sources exactly the footnotes the answer draws, so both blocks pair from one list.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Seven are pinned to a colour family on purpose: accent for the footnote mark and the links to the documents, which can be pressed in a card that is often neutral, and info for the icon over an answer with no sources. The answer's text uses the shared Markdown renderer, listed on Sources.

What we have learned

  • «Kilder brukt i svaret» is in the same order as the documents in Sources, and only lists what the answer cites. Its rows are not numbered: only excerpts are.
  • Models write the footnote after the full stop: 195 of 237 markers in 37 answers from genki-test on 6 November 2026. Held on the line of the word before, the mark never starts a line alone.
  • Every one of those 237 markers leads to a source shown, and the scan finds 0 leftovers from the source format in the 37 answers. Two answers from 3 November had markers with no source; the block draws those as text.

Open questions

  • In document mode, is a mark with a document in it, named by the title, the right way to name a document in the text?
  • genki-test's services cite documents. Excerpt mode on live answers waits for answers that name their excerpts (digdir/genki#283).
