Footnotes

The answer with its footnotes: each footnote leads to the one excerpt it cites, Sources leads back to it, and «Kilder brukt i svaret» lists the documents under the answer.

Use it when

  • Users must be able to check each claim against its source.
  • Each footnote should lead to what it cites, and each excerpt back to where the answer uses it.

Variants

  • By excerpt (the default): a footnote is a small round mark with its excerpt's number, «Utdrag 1», «Utdrag 2», in the order the answer first cites them, whatever number the service wrote. Needs the contract's markers.
  • By document, while a service cites documents: the same round mark, with the document's number, «Kilde 1», «Kilde 2», in the order the answer first cites it. Footnote N leads to the card in Sources numbered N. Footnotes are always numbers, never an icon.
  • States: a skeleton before the first word and a line while it writes; a stopped answer, whose footnotes say their source never came; a failed one; and a finished answer with no sources, which says so over the text.

The pairing comes from the data, never a guess. A footnote that leads to nothing shown is not drawn, two side by side that lead to one item are one footnote, and a [N] that is no footnote, such as a year, stays text. Each footnote is held on the line of the word before it.

Props

  • text, sources and markers: the answer as the contract sends it. [N] is the marker markers names, or the source with index: N.
  • status: streaming, complete, aborted or failed.
  • failure: why a failed answer failed, from the model, the search, the time, the key, too many questions, a question too long, a refused filter or a thread that is gone. The card says what happened and what to do, in kunnskapsassistenten's words, and offers «Prøv igjen» only when asking again can help. A failure that ends an answer on its way is announced, and lost focus moves to «Prøv igjen». Keep it with the turn, so a reload says the same.
  • onOpenCitation: a footnote was followed, with its index and the item it cites, for Sources' target.
  • target: what to scroll to and focus, where Sources' onShowUse and onReturn lead: a footnote, { citation }, or a title in «Kilder brukt i svaret», { document }, so Escape and «Tilbake til svaret» go back to whichever the reader followed.
  • onOpenDocument: a title in «Kilder brukt i svaret» was chosen. href gives a footnote and a title an address, for a new tab; with neither href nor onOpenCitation, a footnote is a mark, not a link. citationId gives each footnote an id.
  • citationMode: excerpt (the default) or document, the service's choice or the reader's. noAnswer: the text is the service's notice that it found nothing, so no sources and no closing question follow.
  • sourcesUsed (on by default), closingQuestion (off), onRegenerate under a stopped or failed answer, and the actions slot under a finished one.
  • assistantName: who answered, for a screen reader; «KI-assistenten» without it. headingLevel: the level of the answer's own top heading, 3 by default. color sets Designsystemet's data-color on the card, only when given; className is added to the card's own.
  • highlight: search matches to mark in the text, as the conversation's «Søk i svaret» passes them.

answerCitations gives Sources exactly the footnotes the answer draws, so both blocks pair from one list.

answerCopy gives the text for «Kopier svaret», as kunnskapsassistenten copies it: the answer with its footnotes numbered as drawn, and «Kilder» under it, one line per numbered excerpt with its title, page, year and address. Its sources count is the receipt's.

Branding

A brand is a Designsystemet theme. The block sits in @layer genai.components, follows data-color, data-color-scheme and data-size, and reads every token through its own variable, with the token as the fallback. Seven are pinned to a colour family on purpose: accent for the footnote mark and the links to the documents, which can be pressed in a card that is often neutral, and info for the icon over an answer with no sources. The answer's text uses the shared Markdown renderer, listed on Sources.

What we have learned

  • «Kilder brukt i svaret» is in the same order as the documents in Sources, and only lists what the answer cites. Under each title stand the numbers its footnotes carry: «Utdrag 2–3», or «Kilde 1» while the service cites documents.
  • A tinted circle on the card is 1.2:1 in the digdir theme, and 1.1:1 in dark mode, so its edge is all but gone. A ring in the accent's strong border colour is 6.6:1 and 6.2:1; the number on the fill is 11.5:1 in both, and 6.1:1 and 6.3:1 on hover.
  • Models write the footnote after the full stop: 195 of 237 markers in 37 answers from genki-test on 6 November 2026. Held on the line of the word before, the mark never starts a line alone.
  • Every one of those 237 markers leads to a source shown, and the scan finds 0 leftovers from the source format in the 37 answers. Two answers from 3 November had markers with no source; the block draws those as text.

Open questions

  • genki-test's services cite documents. Excerpt mode on live answers waits for answers that name their excerpts (digdir/genki#283).
  • kunnskapsassistenten's reference list starts each line with the publisher. The contract's sources carry none, so the copied list starts with the title.
